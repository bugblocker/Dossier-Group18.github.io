Overview

For our project we interviewed Ketzie Diaz, the Circulation Manager at the Peninsula Center branch of the Palos Verdes Library District (PVLD). She manages 22 employees responsible for circulation services including checkouts, book drops, shelving, and helping patrons sign up for library cards.

Typical Tasks

  • Help patrons check out items
  • Issue library cards
  • Resolve account issues
  • Empty book drops
  • Reshelve materials

Professional Context

Ketzie received her MLIS from San Jose State University and is a member of professional organizations including ALA, CLA, and PLA.

Job Market Outlook

Ketzie currently holds the classification Library Manager III within PVLD.

Technical Skills

  • Microsoft 365 & Google Workspace
  • Library databases
  • Cataloging standards
  • Social media proficiency

Training Agenda

Most librarian positions require a bachelor's degree and an MLIS from an ALA accredited school.

Labor Trends

Library employment has seen steady growth according to labor statistics.

Interview Process

Ketzie asked for us not to share the transcript & recording of her interview; however, we've created a timeline to illustrate our process as well as a sampling of the questions we asked Ketzie.

Interview Questions:
  1. What is your official title and how would you describe it?
  2. What responsibilities fall under your position?
  3. How did you get started in the field?
  4. How long have you worked at PVLD?
  5. Were you always interested in public libraries?
  6. What skills are required for this role?
  7. How has AI impacted your work?
