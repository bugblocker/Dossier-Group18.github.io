Interview Process

Ketzie asked for us not to share the transcript & recording of her interview; however, we've created a timeline to illustrate our process as well as a sampling of the questions we asked Ketzie.

Interview Questions:
  1. What is your official title and how would you describe that to someone outside your field?
  2. What responsibilities fall under your position
  3. How did you get started in the field?
  4. How long have you worked at PVLD?
  5. Were you always interested in working in public libraries?
  6. Is a niche background crucial in this job market, or is it more advantageous to be more well rounded?
  7. If you weren’t a circulation manager/ librarian what kind of librarian LIS professional would you be?
  8. In what ways are you engaging with the public?
  9. What questions are you commonly asked/ fielding?
  10. What are some new items/materials/collections you are working with?
  11. What is your day to day like?
  12. How often are you working alone versus collaborating with peers?
  13. How often are you collaborating with professionals outside of your library?
  14. How often are you collaborating with individuals outside of LIS Professions?
  15. What is the hierarchical structure of your department?
  16. Is it hard to get things approved through the chain of command?
  17. Do you find that there is room for growth within the organization?
  18. Are you a member of any professional organizations?
  19. Do you attend conferences?
  20. Do you think specialization in MLIS programs is needed?
  21. Did you take any courses that prepared you for your current role?
  22. What is the most difficult part of this position?
  23. What kind of skills are needed?
  24. Do you think skills requirements will change?
  25. What skills outside LIS are important?
  26. Where did you develop the skills needed?
  27. Has AI impacted your job?
  28. If not, why not?
  29. Do you see AI affecting your job in the future?
  30. Does AI in other professions affect your work?
